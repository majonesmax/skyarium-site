The Horizon

The sky, drawn from where you stand

Your backyard, or a famous spotting spot on the other side of the world, drawn on real terrain, skylines and weather, with the sun and moon where they really are. Every flight is in it where it actually is.

  • Look around by turning your phone, or drag the sky.
  • Tap a plane to see what it is, where it's going and when it passes closest.
  • Follow one all the way down to the runway.
  • Save the ones you like to your Logbook as boarding passes.
The Wall

Skyarium on your wall

Turn your phone on its side while it charges and Skyarium keeps the sky going in the background. The Horizon directs itself, cutting to whatever's landing or taking off, between an arrivals board and a list of everything flying nearby. At home, a chime tells you a plane is about to pass over. Watching somewhere else, it keeps your time as well as theirs.

Free

Free for everyone, the same for everyone

No subscriptions, no ads, no accounts, and nothing locked behind a paywall. Everyone gets the same Skyarium. It's supported by the people who use it: if you love it, you can leave an optional tip in the app. It unlocks nothing; it just keeps the planes flying.
