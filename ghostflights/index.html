Ghost flights

Look for a ghost

Old airlines fly their old routes again after midnight. Northwest Orient and Republic are back over Minneapolis and Western is back over Southern California until about 5 in the morning.

Turn them on in Skyarium under Settings › The Horizon › Ghost flights.

Ghosts fly real routes these airlines flew in their day, between midnight and 5 AM local time. They glow pale blue and say Ghost so you won't mistake one for a real plane. Everyone sees the same ghosts at the same time. Tap a drawing to see its other side. Airline names, logos and liveries belong to their owners.

See them in your own sky

In Skyarium every light overhead is a real flight drawn in its own paint. After midnight a few old ones join them.
