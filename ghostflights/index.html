Ghost flights

Look for a ghost

Eighteen airlines that are gone fly again every night. Their real flights fly at their real times, from the airlines' own timetables of 1985 and 1979.

Turn them on in Skyarium under Settings › The Horizon › Ghost flights.

Ghosts are flights these airlines really flew at that hour. They leave from 9 PM and land by 7 AM local time and are the same flights as on ghostflights.tv. They glow pale blue and say Ghost so you won't mistake one for a real plane. Everyone sees the same ghosts at the same time. Tap a drawing to see its other side. Airline names, logos and liveries belong to their owners.

See them in your own sky

In Skyarium every light overhead is a real flight drawn in its own paint. At night the old ones join them.
